training-my-moe · site/

QwenMoE Explorer

Six pages for a Mixture-of-Experts transformer built from scratch. They are listed in the order that reads best for a first visit: the model at a glance, every tensor of one forward pass drawn to scale, its shape under any configuration, one token going through it, how the design evolved, and what each design choice measurably costs.

Pages

Each page sits in its own folder with its own code and data, so changing one cannot break another. They share one copy of D3 and KaTeX in site/shared/lib/.

2 of 6site/tensor-walk/index.html

What shape is every tensor?Tensor walk

An animated walk through one forward pass, every tensor drawn as a rectangle sized by its real rows and columns while the figure's camera follows along. Swap any part at any moment (norms, positions, GQA/MHA/MQA/MLA, sliding windows, expert shape and type, routing, balancing, per-layer patterns) and the figure and the chain of steps rebuild. Parts not in this repo are marked and cited.

Built by site/shared/qwenmoe-model.js; uv run python scripts/render_tensor_walk.py checks its parameter counts against the real model

Step through a real forward pass of any of seven trained architectures, one operation at a time, with the real activations, attention weights and routing decisions at every step. The walkthrough changes shape per architecture; a QK-norm model has a step that other models do not.

Where each page's content comes from

Pages never compute training results themselves. The generated outputs are committed, so a fresh clone shows every page. Rebuilding needs the inputs in the last column, which are git-ignored because checkpoints and corpora are large; recreate them by retraining. uv run python site/check_site.py confirms every file a page references exists and is tracked.

Everything here opens straight from disk (file://) with no server. Fonts and the map's diagram library come from the internet; without a connection, pages fall back to system fonts and the map shows its timeline, spec sheet and index without the drawing.
